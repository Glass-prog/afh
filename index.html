What We Offer

Our Care Services

We provide comprehensive, personalized care for each resident — tailored to their unique needs, preferences, and dignity.

Personal Care

Bathing, dressing, grooming, and hygiene assistance provided with respect and dignity. Our caregivers support independence while ensuring safety and comfort.

Medication Management

Careful, accurate medication administration and monitoring. We coordinate with physicians and pharmacies to ensure every resident's health regimen is followed precisely.

Meal Preparation

Nutritious, home-cooked meals prepared fresh daily with residents' dietary needs, preferences, and restrictions in mind. Dining is a highlight of each day.

Housekeeping

A clean, tidy, and comfortable living environment is maintained throughout the home. Laundry, cleaning, and upkeep are all handled so residents can relax.

Social Activities

Engaging activities, games, crafts, and outings that keep residents mentally active, socially connected, and joyful. We celebrate birthdays and holidays together.

Transportation Assistance

Help with medical appointments, errands, and outings in the Auburn area. We ensure residents get where they need to go safely and on time.

A True Family Home

Where Generations Live & Thrive Together

What makes Attic Adult Family Home truly special is that it is exactly that — a family home. Samjhana and her family live here alongside their residents, and that includes their two young daughters who bring an irreplaceable joy and energy to everyday life.

Residents enjoy the warmth of a multigenerational household: the laughter of children playing, the liveliness of a real family going about their day, and the deep sense of belonging that only a genuine home can offer. Research consistently shows that intergenerational environments improve mood, reduce loneliness, and bring meaningful stimulation to seniors.

This is not a facility where staff clock in and out. This is someone's home — and your loved one becomes a cherished part of it.
